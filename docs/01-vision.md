# 01 — Vision

## One-line concept
**"The Outlaw's Journal"**: a portfolio you *discover*, not read. Night falls over a camp in the
hills; a leather journal lies by the fire. Open it, and every page is a chapter of
Bodruddoza Araf's story as an engineer: sketched, handwritten, stamped and pinned like the
journal of a Wild West outlaw.

## Why this theme works for Araf
- Arthur Morgan's journal (pencil sketches plus handwritten entries) is one of the most loved
  UI motifs in games. It gives us a strong, recognizable visual language that isn't just
  another dark-mode dev portfolio.
- The "lone outlaw" story maps onto real resume facts: **sole engineer** who took a store from
  empty repo to nationwide orders in **two months**.
- Wanted posters, bounty boards, maps, telegrams and satchels give every standard portfolio
  section (skills, projects, experience, contact) a natural, playful container.

## Audience (in priority order)
1. **Recruiters and hiring managers.** They skim for 30–60 seconds. They need name, role,
   availability (graduating Oct 2026, available full time), top projects and contact, FAST.
2. **Engineers and tech leads.** They'll click into case studies and judge technical depth
   (idempotency, atomic stock, eval metrics, thesis numbers) and the site's own engineering.
3. **Creative-dev community** (Awwwards, X/Twitter, Reddit r/reddeadredemption,
   r/webdev). They share it if the experience is special. This is the "reach" audience.

## Goals
| # | Goal | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Land interviews for full-time SWE / AI / ML roles | Contact form submissions, recruiter feedback |
| G2 | Be memorable: people share the link | Social shares, Awwwards / CSSDA submission |
| G3 | Prove engineering skill through the site itself | Lighthouse ≥ 90, clean repo, well-engineered 3D and motion |
| G4 | Never block the work behind the art | Plain mode; key info visible within 5 s of load |

## Success criteria for v1.0 launch
- All resume projects, experience, research, skills and contact present and accurate.
- Signature moments working: campfire 3D hero, journal opening, wanted poster, bounty board,
  map trail, weapon-wheel nav, Dead Eye easter egg.
- Lighthouse (mobile) Performance ≥ 85, Accessibility ≥ 95, Best Practices ≥ 95, SEO 100.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected; Plain mode available from every page.
- Works on latest Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and mid-range Android.
- Deployed on Vercel at **https://bodruddozaaraf.me** (with `www` redirect).

## Feel / mood keywords
Dusk. Campfire glow. Graphite on aged paper. Ink blots. Leather and brass. Slow, cinematic
camera moves. Quiet wind, crackling fire. Handwritten warmth plus hard technical facts.
**Playful, never cheesy.** Lines like "Wanted: for crimes against legacy code" are fine;
cowboy slang in every sentence is not. Technical content stays precise and professional.

## Non-goals
- Not a game. No required gameplay to reach content.
- Not a blog (can be added later).
- Not a replica of RDR2 UI screens.

## IP rules (important)
RDR2, Arthur Morgan and their visual identity belong to Rockstar Games / Take-Two.
- ❌ No RDR2 logos, wordmarks, screenshots, textures, ripped 3D models, audio, music, or the
  game's proprietary fonts.
- ❌ No depiction of Arthur Morgan's face or likeness. Don't use "Red Dead" in the site title,
  domain or metadata.
- ✅ Generic Wild West / frontier motifs (wanted posters, maps, campfires, horses, telegrams).
- ✅ Original sketch-style art, free-licensed fonts, CC0 textures and models.
- ✅ An "inspired by" nod in the footer/colophon is fine.
- ✅ Short original lines *in the spirit of* the game are fine. Don't copy game dialogue
  verbatim at length.

# /styleguide

Scope: development reference page (noindex, unlinked) that renders every token, face, material
and control of the Outlaw's Journal world. Visitor mode: Read (an engineer or agent checking a
value). It is also the first built proof of the visual world that every later surface inherits.

Audience and job: Araf and future agents confirm tokens, contrast pairs, faces and control
states before building sections. Content is real resume copy from docs/02-content.md, never
lorem. Constraints: colors are parsed from src/styles/tokens.css at build time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The world is brief-pinned (docs/03, D32, D35): an outlaw's leather journal found by a
campfire. This page refuses the default styleguide (white cards of swatches on a flat grid) and
shows tokens as physical material on the page: ledger rows, textured chips, pasted notes.

OWN-WORLD: Aged paper #E9DCC0 with procedural foxing and fibre flecks, iron-gall ink #2A2118,
one night band (#0E0F12 to night blue) lit by ember #E0892B, blood red #8E1B1B for stamps and
focus, pebbled leather #5C3A21. Rye woodtype caps, IM Fell English body, Homemade Apple and
Caveat hands, Special Elite figures. Square corners, warm offset shadows, printed-ticket buttons
with an inner hairline rule.

STORY: The reader sees each material, learns its rule (where it may carry text, at what
contrast), and leaves able to build a section without guessing a value.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A pasted paper-light sheet, rotated -0.4deg with a pinned shadow, top left, holding
the h1 "Styleguide" in Rye at h1 scale, a lead paragraph, and the token file path in type. The
textured page shows around it; the ledger begins below.

FORM: Brief-pinned world (no concept roll: new-work says a user or brief-pinned direction beats
the roll; D35). Surface form: ledger plus specimen sheet, position 1 of 1. Seed key: none
(pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## The Job

A leather goods brand in Dhaka needed a store that could take **cash-on-delivery orders** from
every district, run by one engineer, launched in two months.

### How it was done

Order placement had to stay correct under failure:

- **Idempotency keys** block duplicate orders.
- **Guarded atomic decrements** restore stock exactly once on cancellation.
- **Snapshotted line items** stop later product edits rewriting order history.

> Designed order placement to stay correct under failure.

Delivery pricing runs on the server across all 64 districts. The store is live at
[jackthejelli.com](https://jackthejelli.com).
